Privacy Policy

Short version: your habits stay on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of your data.

Last updated 7 November 2026

Tend has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Who we are

Tend is made by Tom Bettle, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For anything about this policy, email support.tend@icloud.com.

What Tend stores, and where

Your habits, the times you log them, lighter weeks, your weekly reflections and notes, and your reminder time are stored only on your iPhone, in Tend's private storage. Tend's widgets read the same on-device data. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include Tend's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Tend doesn't use analytics, crash-reporting services, advertising, tracking or third-party code that collects data. We can't see your habits, and we don't know who you are.

Purchases

Tend Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your name, card or Apple ID. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you choose a daily reminder, Tend schedules it on your device. It isn't sent from a server, and you can turn it off at any time in Tend's Settings or iPhone Settings.

Children

Tend doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the Tend app and all its data is removed from your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights over your personal data. As we don't collect or hold any, there is nothing to access, correct or export from us. If you have a question, email us and we'll gladly help.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add optional iCloud sync), we'll update this page and the date above, and explain the change in the app's release notes.
